The practical bits

A few things worth knowing.

Have another question? Reach out and ask us directly.

Does sending an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. It sends an enquiry. We’ll discuss availability, arrangements, and pricing. Your booking is confirmed only after you accept the final details.

How much does a tour cost?

Prices depend on the service, date, group size, and itinerary. Contact us with your plans to request a price.

What does the price include?

Inclusions depend on the arrangement. Ask for the agreed services, transport, fees, and exclusions in writing before confirming.

Can I suggest my own route?

Yes. Include your idea in the enquiry and we’ll discuss what may be possible.

What’s the cancellation policy?

Cancellation and change terms depend on the service. Ask which terms apply before confirming.

Can I request accessibility or child-friendly arrangements?

Please mention accessibility or child-related needs in your enquiry. We’ll discuss what can be arranged.
