Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Is my ride confirmed when I send the form?

No. The form sends a price enquiry. Availability, trip details, and pricing should be discussed before your ride is confirmed.

How is the estimated fare calculated?

The estimate uses a €4 start fee, €3 per kilometre, and €0.50 per estimated driving minute. Traffic, waiting time, and route changes may affect the final fare.

Can I add one or more stops?

Yes. Select “Add a stop” to include additional addresses in your enquiry.

Can I request a return journey?

Yes. Select “Return trip” and provide your preferred return pickup date and time.

Is the online price a confirmed quote?

No. It is an estimate based on route information. Please confirm the final fare and ride details with us.
