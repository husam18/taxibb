Before you go

A few helpful answers.

Is my ride confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form sends an enquiry. The ride is confirmed only after availability, details and pricing have been agreed.

How is the online estimate calculated?

The estimate uses a €4 start fee, €3 per kilometre and €0.50 per estimated driving minute. Traffic and route changes may affect the final fare.

Can I add stops and request a return ride?

Yes. Add stops and select the return ride option.
