Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Contact us if you have a question that isn’t answered here.

Does sending the form confirm my tour?

No. It sends an enquiry. We’ll contact you to discuss availability, arrangements, and pricing. Your booking is confirmed only after you accept the final details.

How much does a tour cost?

Prices depend on your requested service, dates, group size, and itinerary. Contact us with your plans to request a price.

What is included in a tour?

Inclusions vary by arrangement. Before confirming, ask us to provide the agreed services, transport, fees, and any exclusions in writing.

Can I request a custom route?

You can include your idea in the enquiry. We’ll discuss whether it can be arranged and provide details before you decide.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation and change terms depend on the service. Ask for the terms that apply to your proposed trip before confirming.

Can you help with accessibility or children’s needs?

Please describe any accessibility, child-seat, or other requirements in your enquiry. We’ll discuss what can be arranged.
